# Changelog

## Unreleased

- First rig session (2026-10-26): four on-off runs, `WATER-R0001`–`R0004`; report generated by
  `matlab/scripts/level_session_report.m` (cycles, flows, overshoot, the sensor, each run replayed
  in MATLAB and Simulink) in `results/reports/water_level/2026-10-26/`; `docs/RESULTS.md` compares
  it with the predictions.
- Firmware, after the rig: one ping per 100 ms step (pings closer than 60 ms heard each other's
  echoes), the level the median of the last five accepted pings, a ping rejected when it is further
  from that median than the water can move, a sensor fault after 5 s of rejected pings while
  running; pump ON only after 3 readings below Lmin; message texts in flash (RAM 62 % -> 30 %, the
  stack had overrun the telemetry buffer); free RAM in HELLO.
- Simulink: the ON-OFF relay starts with the pump off, as the firmware does (the Relay block
  starts in its off state, and the old wiring made off = pump on).
- `matlab/scripts/level_rig_session.m`: a scripted rig session (geometry, idle noise, on-off runs).

- The lab rig: a 15 L jar filled from a 500 L tank by a 0.5 HP-class mains pump switched by a
  Fotek SSR-40DA; the defaults, the wiring, the safety rules and the checklist describe it.
- Firmware: build options PUMP_PWM (0: on-off actuator, default; 1: DC pump on a MOSFET) and
  RELAY_ACTIVE_LOW (default 0: SSR, active HIGH); P and PI refused on an on-off actuator; minimum
  on and off times (3 s, 15 s); no-filling guard in cm per window; run-time guard; overflow also
  within 4 cm of the sensor; calibration kept in EEPROM, `$ZERO` (empty tank) and RUN refused until
  calibrated; `$GRD` sets the guards; AVR watchdog.
- App: pump wiring choice (sets the build), the run check refuses what the wiring cannot do, every
  command acknowledged, a run the firmware stopped ends at once; the simulator mirrors the new guards
  and spills at the rim.

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25): first public release

The Arduino water level control lab, released on its own: the Arduino firmware with its safety cut-offs, the
`water_level_lab` app (missions, a 3-D rig, physics from the parts you describe, the SIM / RIG / REF
table, recording, replay, overlay, OPEN IN SIMULINK), a Simulink model generated from code,
the firmware tested on the PC against an emulated rig, and one-line set-up on Windows, macOS
and Linux with CI on all three. Not yet run on a real rig.
